MembraneForger

All-atom membrane-protein systems for GROMACS, built and checked from one structure file

Simulating a membrane protein starts with a system: the protein in a realistic bilayer, oriented, solvated, with ions, and free of clashes. MembraneForger builds that system from your PDB file. It embeds the protein in a pre-equilibrated Martini 3 cell membrane, backmaps the membrane to all atoms, solvates and minimizes with CHARMM36, and grades every stage before the result is written.

  • Do you have a cryo-EM complex, an AlphaFold model or a docked ligand that needs a membrane?
  • Do you need replicate systems that differ only in their lipid arrangement?
  • Do you want to know, before production, that the membrane and the protein are sound?

One command answers all three. Built for GPCR complexes, it works for any membrane protein that fits the 18 x 18 nm membranes it ships with, or in a Martini 3 membrane of your own. 

pip install git+https://github.com/rolanddelmundo/MembraneForger.git
membraneforger --aa complex.pdb --orient-chain R --nterm-side out --out my_build

Your first build walks through the command, the output and the report.

How a build works

What goes in

  • A PDB of everything to simulate, with unique chain IDs and no water or bulk ions.
  • Optionally a membrane: a bundled frame by number or name, or your own Martini 3 frame.
  • GROMACS on your PATH. PPM 3.0 only if the structure is not oriented and has no OPM entry.

What comes out

  • em.gro, topol.top, toppar/ and an index file: the minimized system, ready to equilibrate.
  • membrane_validation.md, audit.json and run_manifest.json: what was checked, against which thresholds.
  • int/: every intermediate file, for inspection and restarts.

Built on

Martini 3 membranes · mstool backmapping · CHARMM36 and CHARMM-GUI force field and equilibration schedule · CGenFF ligand parameters · GROMACS · OPM and PPM 3.0 orientation.

To cite MembraneForger, use CITATION.cff together with the tools above. MIT licensed; force-field files and mapping data keep their own terms.
