MembraneForger

Turn a protein structure into an energy-minimized, all-atom membrane system that is ready to equilibrate in GROMACS.

Give it a PDB file. It places the protein in a realistic plasma membrane, adds water and salt, minimizes the energy and checks every step.

Get started Input requirements
What you get, and what comes next. MembraneForger stops after energy minimization: atoms are moved to remove clashes, but nothing has moved at temperature yet. Before collecting data you still run a short restrained equilibration (templates included) and then production MD, both in GROMACS.

Quick start

  1. Check the prerequisites

    Linux or macOS, Python 3.10 or later, and GROMACS on your PATH (on a cluster, load its module). A GPCR-sized build takes about 15 to 30 minutes on 8 cores.

  2. Install

    git clone https://github.com/rolanddelmundo/MembraneForger.git
cd MembraneForger
pip install ".[figures]"

  3. Prepare your PDB

    One model, every chain with its own ID, no water or bulk ions. See the checklist below or Preparing a PDB.

  4. Run the pre-flight check

    It reads your inputs as the build will, checks GROMACS and the other programs, and writes nothing. Fix every FAIL line.

    membraneforger --check --aa complex.pdb --orient-chain R --nterm-side out --out my_build

  5. Build the bundled example

    A glucagon receptor complex that is already oriented, so it needs nothing else:

    membraneforger \
    --aa membraneforger/examples/preeq_cg_cellmem/GCGR_MTZP_prot-lig.pdb \
    --cg membraneforger/examples/preeq_cg_cellmem/GCGR_MTZP_cg_cellmem.gro \
    --orientation none --out example_out

  6. Look at the result

    The last line of example_out/membranebuilder.log says PASS or FAIL. What each file is is explained below, and Your first build walks through it.

Is my structure compatible?

  • An all-atom protein structure (PDB) from crystallography, cryo-EM or a model such as AlphaFold, with complete side chains. Missing heavy atoms are not rebuilt, and missing loops are not modelled. Details
  • One model and a unique chain ID for every chain. Remove water, bulk ions, detergents and crystal lipids. Keep structural ions such as Mg2+. Details
  • A known membrane orientation. Either the file is already oriented (membrane normal along z, extracellular side up, as in OPM files), or MembraneForger orients it from its OPM entry or with PPM 3.0. You name the membrane-spanning chain and the side its N terminus is on. Details
  • A topology for every ligand. Small molecules need CHARMM/CGenFF parameters as a GROMACS .itp file. MembraneForger does not generate them. GDP and Mg2+ are bundled. Details
  • A protein that fits the membrane patch. The bundled membranes are 18 x 18 nm, sized for a GPCR. Smaller proteins and your own membranes are supported. Details
  • GROMACS on your PATH. PPM 3.0 is only needed when the structure is not oriented and has no OPM entry.

What comes out

A successful build writes these files to the --out folder (example from the bundled GCGR build):

em.groThe finished system: protein, membrane, water and ions, energy-minimized. It is written only if every check passed. Start equilibration from this file.
topol.top, toppar/The force-field description of every molecule (CHARMM36). GROMACS needs these together with em.gro.
index_ini.ndxAtom groups (protein and ligands, membrane, water and ions) used by the equilibration files.
membrane_validation.mdA readable report on the membrane: packing, composition and thickness, with figures and an overall verdict (PASS, PASS WITH WARNINGS or FAIL).
audit.jsonIndependent checks on the final system: topology, net charge, energy, ring threading and lipid geometry.
run_manifest.json, membranebuilder.logThe exact settings and software versions, and a line-by-line log. Attach both when you report a problem.
int/Intermediate files from every stage, for inspection or restarts.

From structure to simulation

After the build: simulation

The minimized system is the starting point. After restrained equilibration you run production MD in GROMACS, and the protein, ligand and lipids start to move.

How it works

  1. Orientalign the protein to the membrane
  2. Embedplace it in a coarse-grained membrane
  3. Backmaprebuild the lipids with all atoms
  4. Solvateadd water and salt
  5. Minimizeremove clashes

The system is checked after each step. A failed check stops the build and names the step and the file to look at. The individual checks and their thresholds are in Advanced options.

What you can do

Put a membrane protein in a realistic cell membrane

Receptors, G proteins, peptides and ligands keep the pose you give them. The membrane is an asymmetric plasma membrane with cholesterol, sphingomyelin, GM3 and PIP2, equilibrated for 50 µs.

membraneforger --aa complex.pdb --orient-chain R --nterm-side out --out build

Build several independent starting membranes

The same protein in N different bundled membrane snapshots, so each system starts with a different lipid arrangement. These are independent starting configurations. They are not MD replicates of one system, which come from re-running equilibration with new velocities.

membraneforger --aa complex.pdb --cg 2 --runs 5 --orient-chain R --nterm-side out --out build

Use your own membrane, protein size or lipid mix

Embed into any Martini 3 membrane, place small single-pass proteins on intact bilayer, or remove and swap lipid types.

membraneforger --aa complex.pdb --cg my_frame.gro --embed --out build

Run one stage on its own

Stop after slicing or backmapping, or start from a protein and membrane you assembled yourself, with the same checks.

membraneforger solvate --membrane system.pdb --out build

Backmapping: from beads to atoms

The membrane is first simulated coarse-grained: each bead stands for about four heavy atoms, which makes microseconds of lipid mixing affordable. Backmapping rebuilds every lipid with all its atoms on those beads, so the membrane keeps its equilibrated arrangement and gains the detail an all-atom simulation needs. Ring threading, bond crossings and stereochemistry are then checked.

Good to know

  • Minimized is not equilibrated. Run the restrained equilibration (six steps, 1.875 ns) before production MD.
  • Ligand parameters are your responsibility. MembraneForger uses the topology you provide. Check CGenFF penalty scores before trusting a ligand.
  • Passing checks is not proof of biology. The checks catch clashes, broken lipids and badly packed membranes. They cannot tell whether the protein model, its protonation states or the ligand pose are right.
