MembraneForger

One structure file in, a validated all-atom membrane system out.

MembraneForger places your protein or protein–ligand complex in a pre-equilibrated Martini 3 cell membrane, backmaps the membrane to all atoms around it, and returns a solvated, minimized CHARMM36 system for GROMACS that has passed every one of its checks. Built for GPCR complexes; works for any membrane protein that fits.

Get started Source on GitHub 

pip install git+https://github.com/rolanddelmundo/MembraneForger.git
membraneforger --aa complex.pdb --orient-chain R --nterm-side out --out my_build

Building a membrane system should not be the hard part

You have a structure

A cryo-EM complex, an AlphaFold model, a docked ligand. It needs a bilayer whose composition and packing are realistic, oriented correctly, with nothing clashing.

You need replicates

Eighteen independent 50 µs Martini 3 cell membranes are bundled, nine around the kappa opioid receptor and nine around GPR139. One flag builds the same complex in each.

You need to trust it

Every stage grades its own result: area per lipid, composition, thickness, lateral RDF, bond crossings, ring threading, forces. em.gro is written only when every check passes, and the report says why.

Use cases

MembraneForger is one build command, but each of its stages answers a question of its own. The first group builds a complete system; the second uses one stage, with its checks, for your own membrane work. Every card carries the command and links to the page that explains its options.

Build a simulation-ready system

A GPCR complex in a bundled cell membrane

Receptor, G protein, peptide and small-molecule ligands, placed where the frame's own receptor was.

  • Any oriented complex, or one with an OPM entry
  • Ten lipid species, asymmetric leaflets, 0.15 M NaCl
membraneforger --aa complex.pdb --orient-chain R --nterm-side out --out build

The same complex in several membranes

Replicate systems that differ in their lipid arrangement, one directory per frame.

  • Up to nine frames per receptor series
  • Repeatable: name a frame instead of drawing one
membraneforger --aa complex.pdb --cg 2 --runs 5 --orient-chain R --out build

Your own Martini 3 membrane

A coarse-grained frame of your complex (the all-atom structure is fitted onto its CG protein), or any Martini 3 bilayer reused with a new protein.

  • Custom compositions, larger patches, non-GPCR shapes
  • Rotated frames are detected and put back in their box
membraneforger --aa complex.pdb --cg my_frame.gro --embed --out build

A single-pass or small membrane protein

Proteins much smaller than a GPCR go on the unbroken bilayer, away from the receptor hole, with a wider margin.

  • Oriented on the transmembrane helix you name
  • Empty-pocket gate keeps the leaflets intact
membraneforger --aa sp.pdb --orient-residues 152-177 --embed-site free --xy-buffer 3 --out build

Ligands, cofactors and lipidated residues

Small molecules, nucleotides, ions and acylated or prenylated residues keep the pose you give them.

  • CGenFF topologies dropped into one folder
  • Ligand heavy atoms frozen in minimization; E/Z prenyl check
membraneforger check --aa complex.pdb ... # reports every residue and its topology

Equilibrate and grade the trajectory

Six restrained steps with the define switches the topology expects, then a production template and a second set of gates.

  • CHARMM-GUI schedule, 310 K, 1.875 ns
  • Box convergence, APL, thickness, core hydration, diffusion
bash "$(python -c 'import membraneforger, os; print(os.path.dirname(membraneforger.__file__))')/mdp/run_equilibration.sh" build

Use one stage on its own

Start from a 50 µs Martini 3 cell membrane

Eighteen equilibrated coarse-grained frames of an asymmetric ten-species plasma membrane around a GPCR, each 18.3 x 18.3 nm, about 1,300 lipids.

  • Starting points for your own Martini 3 simulations
  • Per-species lipid contacts over 50 µs and leaflet compositions documented
membraneforger --list-membranes # files in membraneforger/examples/preeq_cg_cellmem/

Grade a coarse-grained membrane frame

Any Martini 3 frame is checked before anything is placed in it: periodicity in its box, area per lipid, leaflet composition, thickness, lateral packing.

  • A frame rotated out of its box is detected and rotated back
  • Absolute limits, the first stage of membrane_validation.md; a failing frame stops the build before anything is placed in it
membraneforger --aa complex.pdb --cg my_frame.gro --embed --out build # gate 0 runs first, before embedding

Orient and embed a protein in a bilayer

One rigid transform for the whole complex, from its OPM entry or a local PPM 3.0 run, then lipids pushed aside at the coarse-grained level and an empty-pocket gate.

  • --orientation auto|ppm|none, offline with --opm-file
  • Oriented coordinates and transform kept in int/oriented.pdb and orientation_report.json
membraneforger --aa complex.pdb --orientation ppm --orient-chain R --nterm-side out --out build

Slice a membrane to the protein plus a buffer

A periodic, whole-lipid, anchor-based crop: the window is placed where the composition changes least, the seam is relaxed, and the cut is graded against the uncut membrane.

  • Gate: APL per leaflet, composition, lateral RDF, thickness, tilt
  • Smaller systems with the packing of the big one
membraneforger slice --aa complex.pdb --xy-buffer 2.0 --apl-tolerance 3 --out cut # slice_cg.gro, placed_complex.pdb

Backmap a Martini membrane to all atoms

mstool rebuilds every lipid around the rigid all-atom complex, including GM3 and PIP2; the result is checked before it is used.

  • Ring threading, bond crossings and inventory checks; sugar and inositol stereocentres audited
  • Stops after gate 2 and keeps membrane.pdb at the top level
membraneforger backmap --aa complex.pdb --cg my_frame.gro --out aa # then aa/membrane.pdb

Solvate, add ions and minimize any protein + membrane PDB

Start from an assembled all-atom system of your own: CHARMM36 topology, box sized to the solute, TIP3P water, 0.15 M NaCl by water volume, index groups, restrained then free minimization, independent audit.

  • Needs a CRYST1 record and CHARMM36 lipid names
  • Also the restart path after a backmapping of your own
membraneforger solvate --membrane system.pdb --out build

Change the lipid composition

Remove a species from the bundled membrane, or turn it into another twelve-bead Martini 3 lipid before backmapping.

  • Convertible: POPC, DOPC, POPE, DOPE, POPS, DOPS, PSM
  • Composition change reported per leaflet
membraneforger --aa complex.pdb --dellipid DOPC --addlipid POPC --out build

Validate a membrane system before production

Every build writes a staged report, coarse-grained to minimized: area per lipid two ways, composition and asymmetry, lateral RDFs, thickness, lipids around the protein, core hydration, forces.

  • PASS, PASS WITH WARNINGS, FAIL or INSUFFICIENT SAMPLING, with thresholds in one table
  • Gates 3 and 4 grade the equilibrated trajectory
python -m membraneforger.equilibration --out build --energy box.xvg --frames frame*.gro

How a build works

What goes in

  • An all-atom PDB of everything you want simulated, with unique chain IDs and no water or bulk ions.
  • Optionally a membrane: a bundled frame by number or name, or your own Martini 3 frame.
  • GROMACS on your PATH. PPM 3.0 only if the structure is not oriented and has no OPM entry.

What comes out

  • em.gro, topol.top, toppar/, index_ini.ndx: the minimized system, ready to equilibrate.
  • membrane_validation.md with figures, audit.json, run_manifest.json: what was checked and against which thresholds.
  • int/: every intermediate file, for inspection and restarts.

Built on

Martini 3 for the coarse-grained membranes · mstool for backmapping · CHARMM36 and CHARMM-GUI for the all-atom force field and the equilibration schedule · CGenFF for ligand parameters · GROMACS for minimization and simulation · OPM and PPM 3.0 for orientation.
