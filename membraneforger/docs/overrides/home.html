MembraneForger

From one protein structure to an energy-minimized, all-atom membrane system, ready to equilibrate in GROMACS.

It places the protein in a realistic, asymmetric plasma membrane equilibrated for 50 µs, rebuilds every lipid atom by atom, adds water and salt, minimizes the energy and checks every step.

Get started Build the example 
membraneforger --aa complex.pdb --orient-chain R --nterm-side out --out build

From structure to simulation

One build, start to finish: the GPR139 receptor with the peptide dynorphin A (1–8) and its Gq protein, placed in a bundled 50 µs plasma membrane. Select a picture to see it full screen.

  1. Orientalign the protein to the membrane
  2. Embedplace it in a coarse-grained membrane
  3. Backmaprebuild the lipids with all atoms
  4. Solvateadd water and salt
  5. Minimize and auditremove clashes, check everything

The system is checked after each step. A failed check stops the build and names the step and the file to look at. How each check works.

What you get, and what comes next. MembraneForger stops after energy minimization: atoms are moved to remove clashes, but nothing has moved at temperature yet. Run the included restrained equilibration (six steps, 1.875 ns) and then production MD in GROMACS.

Why use it

Checked at every stage

The membrane is graded before embedding, after slicing, after backmapping and after minimization: areas per lipid, composition, thickness, ring threading, stereochemistry and clashes. A build that fails a check writes no em.gro.

Validation

A realistic plasma membrane

Ten lipid species in asymmetric leaflets, with cholesterol, sphingomyelin, GM3 and PIP2, from 18 independent 50 µs coarse-grained simulations. Receptors, G proteins, peptides and ligands keep the pose you give them.

Bundled membranes

Your protein, your membrane

Build in several membrane frames at once, embed into any Martini 3 membrane, place small single-pass proteins, swap lipid types, or run one stage on its own.

Examples and recipes

Backmapping: from beads to atoms

The membrane is first simulated coarse-grained: each bead stands for about four heavy atoms, which makes microseconds of lipid mixing affordable. Backmapping rebuilds every lipid with all its atoms on those beads, so the membrane keeps its equilibrated arrangement and gains the detail an all-atom simulation needs. All ten species are shown at one scale, beads on the left of each pair and atoms on the right.

Good to know

  • Minimized is not equilibrated. Run the restrained equilibration before production MD.
  • Ligand parameters are your responsibility. MembraneForger uses the topology you provide. Check CGenFF penalty scores before trusting a ligand.
  • Passing checks is not proof of biology. The checks catch clashes, broken lipids and badly packed membranes. They cannot tell whether the protein model, its protonation states or the ligand pose are right.
