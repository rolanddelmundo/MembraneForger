MembraneForger

Build all-atom membrane-protein systems for GROMACS from one PDB file

MembraneForger places your protein in an equilibrated Martini 3 plasma membrane, converts the membrane to CHARMM36 all-atom, adds water and ions, and minimizes. Each step is checked; the build stops if a check fails. 

pip install git+https://github.com/rolanddelmundo/MembraneForger.git
membraneforger --aa complex.pdb --orient-chain R --nterm-side out --out my_build

How it works

What goes in

  • A PDB with unique chain IDs, no water or bulk ions (keep structural ions).
  • Optional: a membrane frame (bundled or your own).
  • GROMACS on your PATH.

What comes out

  • em.gro, topol.top, toppar/: the minimized system.
  • membrane_validation.md, audit.json: check results.
  • int/: intermediate files.

Built on

Martini 3 membranes · mstool backmapping · CHARMM36 and CHARMM-GUI force field and equilibration schedule · CGenFF ligand parameters · GROMACS · OPM and PPM 3.0 orientation.

Cite with CITATION.cff and the tools above. MIT licensed; bundled force-field and mapping data keep their own terms.
