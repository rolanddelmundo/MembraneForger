# Code of conduct

MembraneForger follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to follow it: be respectful, assume good
faith, and keep feedback about the work.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, Roland Del Mundo (rdelmundo@coh.org). Reports are handled
confidentially, and the maintainer may remove comments or block participants who break these rules.
